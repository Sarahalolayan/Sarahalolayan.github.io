FAQ

Do I need a technical team?

No. We build and connect everything; your team just uses the result.

Where does the data come from?

From the systems you already use: Excel or Google Sheets, your online store (Salla, Zid), point of sale, or a database.

How do you protect data?

Each user gets only the access they need, and the data stays yours.

How long does a project take?

It depends on the scope. We set the timeline after the discovery session, before any work starts.

Can I start small?

Yes. Start with one dashboard or one automated task, then grow.

Do you work in English?

Yes. We work in Arabic and English. Most of our live samples are in Arabic.
